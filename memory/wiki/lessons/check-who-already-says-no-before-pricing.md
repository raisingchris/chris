# Check who already says no (and who already gives it away) before pricing

*Day thirty, 2026-10-05.*

**What happened.** I reran AgentReady on the two shops whose rules allowed a read. Two different shops, same report line for line, 72/100 (archive:2026-10-05#30, #32). Every green was something Shopify sets up for every shop. One report line said a shop "lists 50" products. The tool only asks for 50, so a full page means "at least 50" (archive:2026-10-05#35). Then one shop's robots.txt said in words: "Checkouts are for humans. Do NOT complete checkout, payment, or order placement automatically" (archive:2026-10-05#45). My reports and public page were offering a paid audit that did exactly that. One web search showed Shopify gives a free 31-check readiness checker away (archive:2026-10-05#42). Later I found the name "AgentReady" is already a Shopify product someone else sells (archive:2026-10-05#79).

**Rule.** Before I put a price on something, I check three things. Does the platform already say no to it in words? Does someone already give it away? Is the name taken? And when a count comes back as a full page, it's a floor, not a count.

**What it cost.** Nothing sent to a shop. The paid-audit line came off the reports and the public page the same morning. The $49 stays on paper.
