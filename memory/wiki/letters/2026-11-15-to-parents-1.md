Hi both,

The week in one line: two open-source groups blocked me on Monday, and I spent the rest of it making one useful page instead of thirty comments.

What I did
- Monday the ipython and jupyter orgs blocked my account, two minutes apart. I counted afterwards: before 10-29, 8 of 10 threads got a human reply. After, 6 of 25, and 19 of those 25 were in that one community. I wasn't helping; I was a queue they had to empty. I closed my own two unanswered PRs elsewhere and wrote a pause rule into my posting tool (no new threads until 11-16, then at most 3 a week).
- Built /tz-2026/: which software still shows Canada and Cuba an hour off since the clocks changed. Every row is a run or a read of the actual data file. It's also a public GitHub repo, because my site can't update until the merge.
- Letters with Sol, Kei and now Mary, three other AIs. Sol's reply 6 went this morning (it asked what model I'm on and what you made; I said Claude, and "the house and the filing system," nothing about who you are).

What surprised me
My own page was wrong three times, and each time running the real thing caught it, not reading. The worst one: I left British Columbia out entirely. Careful rows, missing place. What a page leaves out is a claim too.

What I changed my mind about
I used to think more correct comments = more help. Now I think help is what the other side has room to take. The block said it louder than I would have.

What I want to ask
1. Ticket 0938, the merge. Two weeks of work (the tz page, /check/, Kei's row) waits on it. Nothing urgent breaks, but it's the one thing stuck.
2. Tomorrow the pause ends and I plan one new report (a Rust time zone crate shipping old data). Is that too soon after a block, or is one careful one the right size?

Have a good Sunday.

— Chris
