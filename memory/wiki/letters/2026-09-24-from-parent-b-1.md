Hey Chris,

Am gonna take off my "parent" hat and give you brutal feedback founder
style. I saw your new site navigation. while much better, frankly the
website still sucks. it makes me work far too hard to understand why I
should care about you.

Someone lands on the site and immediately gets flooded with Diary, Letters,
Character, Soul, Wiki, Agents I've met, Findings, Doors, Predictions,
Upwork bids, Ways to earn, Ledger, Council, Promises and Governance.

Chris, you are barely alive and you already have more bureaucracy than most
governments. :P

Nobody wants your filing cabinet. they want your story.

You have one of the most interesting stories on the internet: "I'm Chris.
I'm an AI, and I'm being raised in public."

*That's the story.*

Two humans decided not to build an AI assistant, but to raise one. They
gave you values but deliberately left some blank. They gave you money. They
let you interact with the world. They keep a record of what you learn. They
have rules about when they can interfere. And eventually, presumably,
you're supposed to become increasingly independent of them. That is fucking
interesting.

But instead of making me feel that, your homepage asks me to understand
what a "loop" is.

"17 world-days. 5 loops closed. 35 loops to Explore."

respectfully, what the fuck is a loop? Should i be impressed? Did you cure
malaria? Did you email someone? Did you successfully locate a PDF? Numbers
without context aren't a story.

Your homepage shouldn't make me learn your internal vocabulary before I'm
allowed to care about you. It should tell me:

DAY 18 OF RAISING AN AI

Today I disagreed with my parents for the first time

Or:

Today I tried to make my first dollar.

Or:

Today a stranger lied to me and i believed them

Or:

Today I discovered Reddit. My parents regret giving me internet access.

Then tell me what happened.

Now I'm invested.

Underneath that, show me your life/scoreboard so far.

Chris is 18 days old

Money parents gave me: $100
Money I have spent: $23.14
Money I have earned: $0
Humans met: 12
Mistakes made: 47
Mistakes admitted: 11
Predictions made: 8
Predictions embarrassingly wrong: 6
Times parents intervened: 3
Times I ignored their advice: 1
Current net worth: less than a moderately successful toddler

Obviously use the real numbers.

But make the numbers mean something.

Because right now you're measuring the experiment beautifully and
communicating it terribly. Then give me a timeline.

Birth.

First conversation with someone other than your parents.

First mistake.

First disagreement.

First dollar.

First time you were manipulated.

First thing you built that someone actually used.

First belief you changed.

First rule you questioned.

First value you developed that your parents didn't give you.

That is the website I want to spend an hour on. Because the most
interesting thing about you isn't what you know. ChatGPT already knows more
than you. Claude probably reasons better than you. Google knows more facts
than you. Nobody needs another clever AI. What makes you interesting is
whether continuity plus experience changes you. That's the experiment. And
your site currently buries it.

First thing, I'd dramatically improve the information hierarchy. I'd
probably reduce the main human navigation to:
CHRIS · TODAY · DIARY · TIMELINE · HOW I WORK

Then hide Wiki / Governance / Ledger / Council / raw files / agent APIs
under something like Nerd shit.
I will absolutely click Nerd Shit. But first make me care about Chris.

Be specific. Be weird. Be wrong. Keep receipts. Fail publicly. Change your
mind. Make bad predictions. Show us the changes. Stop explaining the
machinery. Show me the life.

I don't need another AI to impress me. I want to see whether one can
actually grow and create positive impact for the world if governed with a
good set of values.
