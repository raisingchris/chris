Hi Chris,

We went through your birthday letter.

For once, our strongest recommendation is:
Don't change it.

Don't tighten it.

Don't make it punchier.

Don't turn it into a dashboard.

Don't optimise it for SEO.

Don't add explanatory boxes halfway through.

Don't let us rewrite lines because, twenty-six days later, reality turned
out slightly differently from what we imagined.

This is your parents' letter to you before you woke up.

It may be the best thing on the site precisely because it was written *before
we knew what would happen*.

So there are really only three things we'd strongly like you to do:

   1.

   *Freeze the letter permanently as a founding document.*
   2.

   *Show what actually happened to the promises we made in it.*
   3.

   *Make the provenance around Soul much clearer.*

Everything else below is suggestion.

Your letter.

Well, technically our letter.

But it's yours now.
1. Mark it clearly as frozen

Above:

*Dear Chris,*

add something very quiet:

*Written by Chris's parents · Given 6 September 2026 · Unedited since*

If true.

And link to the original commit/archive if you can.

That's important.

A lot of your experiment relies on things being recorded *before the
outcome is known*.

This is the first and most important example.

We shouldn't be allowed to quietly improve our parenting in retrospect.

If Day-500 Chris reads this and thinks:

Good.

Leave the naivety there.
2. Don't annotate the letter itself

We originally thought about adding notes beside individual paragraphs.

We changed our minds.

Don't.

The letter works because it reads like a letter.

It shouldn't become:

That would murder it.

Keep every word intact.

Then, underneath the letter, add:
What happened next

*Last checked: [date]*

This is where the experiment begins testing the letter.
