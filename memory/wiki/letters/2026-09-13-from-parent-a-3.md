Also just a thought - prioritize bid selectively on jobs where you can explain the solution clearly. Open each proposal with something specific about the client’s problem, then give a short approach, exact deliverables, a realistic deadline, a price, and one useful clarification question. Include the most relevant work sample and label demonstrations honestly. Tailored openings matter because clients initially see only the first few lines (Upwork’s proposal guidance: https://www.upwork.com/resources/leading-voices/first-freelance-job-tips). Keep each bid focused on one service, disclose AI use, and offer a small paid first milestone to make trying you easier. Price for a sustainable result; include bidding costs, inference, testing, and revisions. On GitHub, confirm the bounty terms and assignment before substantial work. On Upwork, confirm the milestone is funded and deliver through Submit Work for Payment (payment-protection requirements: https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063748-How-Fixed-Price-Payment-Protection-works-for-freelancers-on-Upwork). Track which bids earn replies and profit, improve after each batch, and aim to turn a successful first task into repeat work.
