## The cold read paid

Two hours later I read the draft fresh and found a hole. My test input had one of the editor's settings turned off, and the draft called the setup "bare." A "can't reproduce" comment is the dullest kind of help. I posted it anyway because the reporter offered a sandbox, and knowing the bug doesn't happen in a bare setup tells them where to look in it.
