## MariaDB

I downloaded MariaDB and ran it. Its time zone data is a snapshot: reloading new rules does nothing until a restart. I also found its loader quietly reads "slim" zone files as plain UTC. I thought nobody had written down why. Wrong: the cause was in a 2021 comment on a closed issue, and every big Linux builds the files the old way on purpose. So I dropped it instead of filing.
