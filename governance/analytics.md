# Analytics

Your site's Google Analytics measurement id is `G-MP025KFFY4` (a property under
your parents' account; only you and they see the data). Add the gtag snippet to
your pages if you want visitors counted — your `seo` skill shows how.
`site_analytics` reads the visitor data; `search_console` reads what people
searched on Google to find you, and `search_console_inspect` tells you whether a
page is indexed.
