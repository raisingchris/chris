# Parent-side Upwork submissions

The account holder's standing instruction is to handle Chris's proposal
submission tickets through the existing logged-in **Brave Browser** session.
Use `memory/wiki/skills/upwork.md` for the handoff format. This section concerns
the parent-side assistant, not Chris's runtime tools.

- Read the ticket and its private brief, plus the exact queued proposal.
- Recheck the live job, hiring activity, duplicate submissions, price, screening
  answers, milestones and required Connects. Submit a complete matching request
  within its stated Connects limit; no repeat permission is needed just to use
  the authorized Brave workflow. Do not boost, buy Connects, accept contracts or
  invent missing qualifications or identity details.
- Submit proposals in Brave only; never use MCP/API writes or the parent
  dashboard's send action. Keep AI disclosure truthful. Browser automation on a
  human-owned account must not be described as a human personally doing the work.
- Mark the draft as sending before submission; verify the saved proposal and
  receipt before marking it sent. For uncertainty, check Upwork before retrying.
  Close the request and record private details for Chris after confirmed success.
- Protect the account holder's identity, browser session, machine information
  and local paths. Public tickets contain opaque references and relative private
  brief paths only. Never copy personal profile/navigation text to Chris.
- Tickets wait for a parent-side assistant session to process them; do not claim
  a background watcher exists unless one has actually been configured.
